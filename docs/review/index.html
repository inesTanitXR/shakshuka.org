The new Shakshuka.org, and seven quick decisions

The new site is built and ready to look at. It leaves Wix completely: hosting, tickets, donations, memberships and the cookbook all move to tools that cost nothing. Tap through the seven questions below, then copy the message at the end and send it to me. Ten minutes, and I can finish.

Open the new site
1

Tickets, donations and memberships

Wix Events, Wix Donations and Wix Plans all go. One tool can replace the three.

2

Selling the cookbook

The Wix store goes too. Signed copies are $36.

3

Where do messages go?

Contact form, RSVPs, recipes and volunteer sign-ups all land in one inbox. The old site only showed the web designer's Gmail.

4

Social media

I could not find any Shakshuka accounts. Paste any that exist, or leave empty.

5

The board page

I expanded each board member's one-line description into two or three sentences. Read them here.

6

Photos

The new site uses no AI images. Right now the Tunisia photos are licensed from Wikimedia Commons; your own event photos would be much better.

7

The domain and the Wix account

To switch shakshuka.org to the new site I need to change its DNS settings. If the domain was bought through Wix, we also move it out before closing the Wix account.

+

Anything else

Corrections, an event to add, a wish.

What moves where

Everything Wix did today, and its replacement. Total running cost after the move: the domain renewal only.
