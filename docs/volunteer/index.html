Volunteer

Bring a dish, bring a skill

Shakshuka is run entirely by volunteers. Whether you have one evening a season or a few hours a week, there is a place for you.

Where we need help

  • Events: check-in, setup, hosting, photography
  • Kitchen: chefs and home cooks for classes and the Iftar
  • Stories: writing, social media, translation (Arabic, French)
  • Back office: bookkeeping, grant writing, membership
  • Music: musicians and singers for the sing-alongs
ShakshukaReimagining community

A Tunisian-American community and cultural initiative in Washington, DC, Maryland and Virginia. A registered 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 92-3134851.
