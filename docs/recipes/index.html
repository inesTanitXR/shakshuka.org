Call for recipes

Your grandmother's recipe belongs in a book

We are building a Tunisian recipe collection for the Shakshuka community in the DC area and beyond. Tell us the region it comes from, the season or occasion, where to find the ingredients in the US, and the story behind it.

Recipes go through review and testing before publication. By submitting you allow Shakshuka to publish the recipe with your name.
