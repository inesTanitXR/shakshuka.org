The cookbook

Discovering Tunisian Cuisine: A Journey of Food and Culture

By Judith Dwan Hallet, Raoudha Guellali Ben Taarit and Hasna Trabelsi. Photographs by Stanley Ira Hallet. First edition 2019, 147-page stitched hardcover, signed.

More than a cookbook: a personal journal of six decades spent in Tunisia, pairing recipes with the landscapes and the people behind them. Brik, couscous, mechouia, the Mediterranean staples of lemon, tomato, pepper and herb, and the kitchen essentials hard to find in America, such as preserved lemons and oumelleh pickles, all in American measurements.

“Awesome and authentic, both visually and content-wise.”Joan Nathan, James Beard and Julia Child Award-winning food writer
