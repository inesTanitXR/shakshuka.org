Cooking Class 5: Couscous & Mloukhia

Couscous is the Friday dish, the wedding dish, the dish your grandmother judges you on. In this live class Chef de cuisine Salma Sellami walks you through steaming couscous the traditional way, a lamb and vegetable stew, and the deep-green mloukhia that takes patience and rewards it.

You'll receive a shopping list and prep notes a week before. Recordings are shared with registered participants.
