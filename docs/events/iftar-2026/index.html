2026 Iftar Dinner and Celebration

Each Ramadan we break the fast together. The 2026 Iftar brought more than a hundred guests to Phoenicia for lentil soup, mohammara and baba ghanouj, fattoush, falafel and grilled mains, followed by live music. As always, the table was open to everyone, whatever their background or belief.
