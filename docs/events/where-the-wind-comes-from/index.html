Free Film Screening: Where the Wind Comes From

Alyssa and Mehdi set off for southern Tunisia to enter a competition that could change everything. Director Amel Guellaty's first feature mixes road movie and daydream to show a corner of Tunisia rarely seen on screen. The screening at the Embassy of France was followed by a conversation with the filmmaker.
