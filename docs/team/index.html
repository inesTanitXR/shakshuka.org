Team Shakshuka

Connection. Knowledge. Passion. Commitment.

A group of volunteers committed to a sense of community, connectedness and a deep appreciation for Tunisia, and to keeping the door open for whoever wants to join.

Leila Chennoufi

President

Born in Tunisia and based in Washington, Leila has spent two decades in sustainability and diaspora work. She founded Shakshuka to give Tunisians of the DMV, and everyone who loves Tunisia, a place to reconnect through culture rather than politics.

Nour Bouzouita

Secretary

Born in Tunisia, Nour works in economics and policy. She keeps the organization's records, runs membership and makes sure every newcomer gets a warm welcome.

Aoufa Ezzine

Treasurer

An engineer by training with a career in international development, Aoufa is also a musician. She manages the books and leads the music behind our sing-alongs.

Sadok Rouai

Board member

Economist and banker with years at the Central Bank of Tunisia and the International Monetary Fund. Sadok brings institutional memory and a deep love of Tunisian history.

Hella Akrout

Board member

Hella imports extra-virgin olive oil from her family's grove in Tunisia. She leads our food programs and the Tunisian cookbook project.

Radhia Jihane Guizani

Board member

A Cap Bon native passionate about dance, film and cuisine. Radhia curates our film screenings and cultural evenings.

Raed Gnaoui

Board member

Born in the United States to Tunisian parents, Raed is a father and soccer enthusiast who makes sure the second generation sees itself in what we do.

Ines Said

Board member

Immersive artist and XR technologist from Nabeul, founder of Tanit XR. Brings heritage, technology and the next generation to the table.

Want to help run things?

We need hands for events, photography, social media, translation and bookkeeping. A few hours a month makes a difference.
